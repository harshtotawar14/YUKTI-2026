01 — Problem

Workers exist. Demand exists. The operating network is missing.

Cooperative labour is skilled, but discovery, trust, opportunity allocation and operational visibility remain fragmented.

01

Discovery Gap

Skilled cooperative workers remain difficult for customers to discover digitally.

02

Trust Gap

Customers lack consistent verification and service-start assurance before work begins.

03

Allocation Gap

Nearest-only matching can ignore eligibility, fairness, availability and worker choice.

04

Management Gap

Cooperatives need unified booking, SLA, capacity, payment and workforce visibility.

Eligibility first. Explainable ranking second. Worker choice always.

04 — Service Lifecycle

One service journey from request to auditable outcome.

Each transition is role-controlled. Future actions remain unavailable until the current trust or completion condition is satisfied.

Request→Worker Accepts→Travel→Arrive→Sandbox Identity→Customer Confirmation→Service→Completion→Sandbox Payment→Invoice→Rating
No forced assignmentCustomer approves additional workNo service start before both trust checksAudit trail preserved
