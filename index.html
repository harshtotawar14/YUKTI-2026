The Problem

Cooperative labour is skilled — but digitally fragmented

Customers need trust, workers need fair access to demand, and cooperatives need one operational view instead of disconnected records and manual coordination.

01

Trust Gap

Customers cannot easily verify identity, skill or current worker verification before service.

02

Discovery Gap

Verified cooperative workers have limited digital visibility while nearby demand remains fragmented.

03

Allocation Gap

Nearest-only allocation can ignore eligibility, fairness, availability and worker choice.

04

Management Gap

Workers, bookings, complaints, capacity, payments and planning are often handled separately.

Why SanPaid Is Different

Designed around cooperative governance, not platform control

Booking, payment and ratings are standard features. SanPaid's differentiation is how trust, fair opportunity, capacity and governance work together.

Cooperative Governance

Verification, complaints, capacity and operations stay accountable to cooperative/federation roles.

Eligibility-First Matching

Trust and safety checks happen before ranking.

Fair Opportunity

Distance, rating and workload/opportunity matter without forcing acceptance.

Cross-Cooperative Capacity

Shortage can create governed capacity requests and voluntary worker offers.

Workforce Planning

Demand and eligible capacity lead to governed operational and training actions.
