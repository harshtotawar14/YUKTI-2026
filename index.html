The Problem

Cooperative labour is skilled — but digitally fragmented

Customers need trust, workers need fair access to demand, and cooperatives need one operational view instead of disconnected records and manual coordination.

01

Trust Gap

Customers cannot easily verify who is arriving, whether the worker is skilled, or whether credentials remain valid.

02

Fragmented Discovery

Verified cooperative workers have limited digital visibility while nearby customers struggle to discover them quickly.

03

Inefficient Allocation

Nearest-only allocation can ignore eligibility, availability, workload balance, worker choice and cooperative capacity.

04

Management Blind Spots

Worker data, bookings, complaints, capacity, payments and workforce planning are often scattered across separate processes.

The SanPaid Solution

Five pillars connect service delivery with workforce governance

The prototype is intentionally focused on the operating system cooperatives need — not only another booking marketplace.

01

Connect

One cooperative service marketplace for customers, workers, societies and federations.

02

Trust

Identity, skill and document verification plus ratings, service history and dual service-start confirmation.

03

Allocate Fairly

Eligibility first, explainable ranking second, and worker choice preserved through Accept / Decline.

04

Protect & Grow

Verified work history, earnings visibility and a research-backed roadmap for training, welfare, insurance and renewal alerts.

05

Govern & Predict

Command Center, complaint SLA, capacity exchange and demand → capacity → skill-gap planning.

Why SanPaid Is Different

Designed around cooperative governance, not platform control

Booking, payment and ratings are useful features — but they are not the innovation. SanPaid's differentiation is how trust, opportunity, capacity and governance work together.

Cooperative Governance

Verification, complaints, capacity and workforce operations stay accountable to cooperative/federation roles.

Eligibility-First Matching

Trust and safety checks happen before any ranking score is allowed to matter.

Fair Opportunity Allocation

Distance, rating and recent workload/opportunity are considered without forcing a worker to accept.

Cross-Cooperative Capacity

Shortage can create governed capacity requests and voluntary worker offers across societies.

Workforce Planning

Demand and eligible capacity lead to operational actions such as capacity requests and training recommendations.

Four Stakeholders

One network, different responsibilities

👤 Customer

Verified local services, transparent approvals, trusted service start and digital history.

🛠️ Worker

Fair job offers, Accept / Decline choice, multilingual request access and transparent earnings.

🏢 Cooperative

Verification, bookings, complaints, workforce capacity and operational analytics.

🌐 Federation

Regional capacity coordination, escalation visibility, training needs and policy governance.
