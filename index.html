The Problem

Cooperative labour is digitally invisible

Skilled local workers exist in every neighbourhood — but demand, trust and fair opportunity don't reliably connect.

01 — DISCOVERY

Fragmented workforce pools

Cooperative societies operate as isolated labour pools with no shared, searchable network — customers can't find them, and workers can't find each other's overflow demand.

02 — TRUST

Registration ≠ ongoing proof

A one-time sign-up doesn't confirm the worker who arrives is the worker who was assigned, or that their skills and documents are still valid today.

03 — FAIRNESS

Nearest-worker isn't fair

Simple proximity matching ignores workload balance, worker choice and eligibility — a few workers get flooded while equally qualified peers sit idle.

How SanPaid Works

From request to rating, every step is auditable

A real sequence, not a marketing list — this is the exact state machine a booking moves through.

01

Request Service

Describe the problem by text, photo or voice, and share your location.

02

Find Verified Worker

Eligibility gate filters by skill, verification and availability; fair ranking scores the rest.

03

Worker Accepts

No worker is ever forced — a reject falls straight through to the next eligible candidate.

04

Verify at Arrival

Sandbox liveness check plus a booking-specific dynamic QR you confirm in person.

05

Start Service

Work begins only once both identity verification and your confirmation are in.

06

Secure Payment

Itemized bill, approved-only extra charges, sandbox payment, instant invoice.

07

Rate & Review

Your rating updates the worker's live score — never the only signal of trust.
