How SanPaid works

Simple for customers. Accountable for cooperatives.

The detailed service record stays connected while the customer journey remains easy to understand.

  1. 1Request ServiceSelect a service, add location and time, describe the problem and attach a photo or voice note when useful.
  2. 2Fair Match & Worker ChoiceEligibility is checked first, suitable verified workers are ranked fairly, and the worker chooses to accept or decline.
  3. 3Estimate → Verify → Service → PayApprove the estimate, confirm the booked worker, complete service, pay securely in the review flow and receive the invoice.

Why SanPaid

Built for local service today—and stronger capacity tomorrow.

SanPaid combines trusted service delivery with cooperative-owned workforce coordination.

◎Local First

Local opportunity before network overflow.

Suitable verified workers from the customer’s local cooperative area are considered first.

⇄Cooperative Capacity Exchange

Shortage? Share verified capacity.

If local capacity is low, a nearby cooperative can support the request—with customer choice, worker consent and authorized responsibility.

Local gap→Worker consent→Authorized support
✓Continuous Trust

Trust stays connected to the service.

Reviewed worker profile, approved estimate, service-start confirmation, billing and complaint ownership remain traceable.
