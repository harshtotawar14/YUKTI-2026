01 — WHY SANPAID

The problem is bigger than booking a worker.

PS 26089 needs a governed cooperative service network: trusted discovery, fair opportunity, transparent delivery, operational visibility and a way to convert recurring shortages into workforce action.

01 · LOCAL DISCOVERY

Trusted nearby workers are hard to find

Customers need a reliable way to discover suitable verified cooperative workers in their area.

02 · PRICING TRANSPARENCY

Similar jobs can receive different quotes

Inspection, estimate approval and an itemized invoice are needed to keep changing job scope transparent.

03 · TRAINING & AWARENESS

Digital adoption needs support

Guided onboarding, local-language flows and skill-readiness support help cooperatives and workers adopt the system.

04 · WORKER WELFARE

Insurance coverage is uneven

Welfare and insurance status should be visible without claiming integrations that are not yet officially connected.

05 · TRUST & SAFETY

In-home service needs identity assurance

Verification must continue into the service-start moment, not stop at profile registration.

03 — LOCKED SERVICE WORKFLOW

Customer control is built into the service chain.

A booking does not jump from matching straight to payment. The worker can inspect, the customer approves price, service start is booking-linked, and completion is customer-confirmed before the final bill.

Problem Details→ Eligible Recommended Workers→ Customer Selects→ Worker Accepts→ Call / Inspection→ Estimate→ Customer Approval→ QR-Verified Start→ Service→ Worker Marks Complete→ Customer Confirms→ Itemized Bill→ Payment→ Invoice→ Rating / Feedback
No forced worker assignmentThe customer chooses from suitable workers; the selected worker still accepts or rejects.
No silent price increaseAny new work or cost requires a revised estimate and fresh customer approval.
No unilateral completionWorker marks complete, customer confirms, then the final bill is generated.

04 — FOUR CONNECTED ROLES

Clear responsibility at every handoff.

The cooperative creates the trusted operating environment; it does not manually control every normal booking. Federation remains primarily a network-governance and exception layer.

CUSTOMER

Choose, approve, verify, confirm

  • Create service request
  • Select a suitable worker
  • Approve original/revised estimate
  • Confirm service start and completion
  • Pay, receive invoice and rate
WORKER

Accept, inspect, estimate, deliver

  • Receive suitable opportunities
  • Accept or reject without forced assignment
  • Inspect and create itemized estimate
  • Deliver approved scope
  • View feedback and trust history
COOPERATIVE ADMIN

Verify, govern and strengthen capacity

  • Worker verification and membership
  • Booking and SLA visibility
  • Complaint ownership and audit
  • Trust / Green Tick review
  • Capacity and skill-gap action
FEDERATION / NETWORK

Coordinate by exception

  • Cross-cooperative policy
  • Regional capacity intelligence
  • Escalated dispute visibility
  • Network-level planning
  • No approval bottleneck on every job
