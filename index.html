The Problem

Cooperative labour is digitally fragmented

Demand, trust, worker opportunity and cooperative planning are often handled separately. SanPaid connects them into one auditable service lifecycle.

01 — Discovery

Customers cannot easily discover verified cooperative workers, while workers can miss nearby demand.

02 — Trust

One-time registration does not prove that documents remain valid or that the arriving worker is the assigned worker.

03 — Fairness

Nearest-only allocation can ignore workload balance, worker choice, skill eligibility and cooperative capacity.

Who It Serves

Four stakeholders, one network

👤 Customer

Verified local services, controlled pricing, service-start confirmation and digital history.

🛠️ Worker

Fair job offers, accept/reject choice, transparent earnings and verified service history.

🏢 Cooperative

Worker verification, bookings, complaints, capacity and command-center analytics.

🌐 Federation

Cross-cooperative capacity exchange, regional SLA view and demand planning.
