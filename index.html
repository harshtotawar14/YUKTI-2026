02 — Current Gap

Skilled cooperative workers exist — trusted digital coordination is fragmented

Customers need verifiable trust, workers need fair access to opportunities, and cooperatives need one operational view for assignments, complaints, capacity and workforce decisions.

01

Trust Gap

Identity, skill and current verification are difficult to confirm before service.

02

Discovery Gap

Verified cooperative workers have limited digital visibility while local demand remains fragmented.

03

Opportunity Gap

Nearest-only logic can ignore eligibility, workload balance, availability and worker choice.

04

Governance Gap

Bookings, complaints, payments, capacity and planning are often managed in disconnected records.

03 — Working Differentiator

SanPaid keeps verification, opportunity allocation, worker consent, capacity coordination and workforce planning under cooperative/federation governance.

06 — Governance & Planning

Decision support — not dashboard decoration

Cooperative Admins should see what requires action: capacity gaps, SLA risk and opportunity distribution. Federation Admins focus on cross-cooperative demand, capacity and policy visibility.

Capacity Gap

Demand exceeds currently available verified capacity. Review local options before requesting governed cross-cooperative support.

SLA Risk

Services approaching configured SLA thresholds should be reviewed before escalation. No fake SLA results are claimed.

Worker Opportunity Distribution

Review whether opportunities are concentrated among too few eligible workers; fairness never overrides eligibility.

Historical Demand→Baseline→Trend Analysis→Forecast→Capacity Planning

Baseline First → ML Later. Early-stage pilot estimates remain baseline-led until enough historical demand data exists. No prediction-accuracy percentage is claimed.

07 — Measurable Impact

Define the baseline first. Measure before claiming impact.

SanPaid does not invent income, speed or satisfaction percentages. Pilot outcomes are measured against a documented baseline.

Customers

Workers

Eligibility-based opportunities, explicit Accept / Decline choice, work visibility and traceable earnings records.

Cooperative Admin

Unified verification, assignments, payments, complaints, SLA and capacity decisions.

Federation / Community

Cross-cooperative capacity coordination, skill-gap visibility and regional workforce planning.
